Location reference data
=======================

Bangladesh divisions, districts, and upazilas are bundled from Open Admin Data's Bangladesh Administrative Divisions dataset, licensed CC BY 4.0:
https://github.com/open-admin-data/bangladesh-administrative-divisions

Country names are bundled from country-list by Saša Stamenković, licensed MIT:
https://github.com/umpirsky/country-list

Country codes follow ISO 3166-1 alpha-2:
https://www.iso.org/iso-3166-country-codes.html
